This portfolio site highlights selected work, experience, and writing. It focuses on fast load times, clean typography, and polished interactions so visitors quickly understand my capabilities.

## Highlights

- Fully responsive with tailored layouts for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Theme-aware components with subtle motion powered by Framer Motion
- Optimized image delivery through Next.js built-in tooling
- Dynamic content sourced from MDX for blogs and project case studies

## Role & Responsibilities

I led the design, implementation, and deployment. That included component architecture, content structure, automated image optimization, and deployment pipelines.
