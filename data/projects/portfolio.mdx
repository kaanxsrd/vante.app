This is the site you're on right now. It's where I introduce myself, show my tech stack and projects, and publish longer posts about the things I build.

## Highlights

- Blog posts and project pages written in MDX and loaded with Fumadocs, with a table of contents, linkable headings and a "read more" section matched by tags
- Generated Open Graph images for the blog, so every post has a proper preview when shared
- A live "now playing" widget backed by the Spotify API
- A contact form that sends email through a Next.js route handler
- Light and dark themes, subtle motion with Framer Motion, and a layout that works from phone to desktop

## Role & Responsibilities

I designed and built the whole site: the layout and components, the content setup for the blog and projects, and the API routes behind the contact form and the Spotify widget.
